DRAYAYA: Raise a Dragon Fruit Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DRAYAYA: Raise a Dragon Fruit (the “App”) handles information about you. The developer information is shown on the App Store product page (the “Developer”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This Policy applies when you use the App, save game records or voluntarily contact us through the available support channels.

We value personal-information and privacy protection. We handle information according to the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, necessity, good faith, transparency, specified purposes and data minimization. Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. If you do not agree with it, stop using the App.

1. Current Data-Handling Overview

  1. The current version does not require an account and does not provide a developer-controlled account system, social relationships, online leaderboards, cloud saves, online matches or cloud synchronization for game data.
  2. The current version provides an in-App language switch. Your language choice and related display preferences are mainly stored locally and are not uploaded to us for that purpose.
  3. The current version does not create a developer-controlled service for synchronizing game data and does not actively upload your scores, game records, device content or other personal information to our servers.
  4. The current version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, user profiling, cross-app tracking, personalized advertising or social-login services.
  5. The App may offer membership subscriptions or other paid benefits through Apple StoreKit. Payment accounts, bank-card numbers, payment passwords and complete payment credentials are handled by Apple and are not obtained by the App.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Local best score, language and settings

To show your result the next time you open the game, the App may store the following locally through iOS local storage:

  1. Your best score;
  2. Your App language, such as Simplified Chinese or English, and related display preferences;
  3. Necessary local state used to display or update the best score and settings.
  4. The local daily-play count and the date associated with that count, so the App can calculate the remaining free plays for the current day.

This information is used to preserve and display your local score, remember your language choice and display preferences, calculate the daily free-play allowance, and display App content. Membership status is obtained and updated from Apple StoreKit responses rather than from a developer account system. This information does not include your name, phone number, email address, account or precise location. The App does not actively upload it to us, make it public to other users or use it for advertising profiles.

2.2 Apple StoreKit purchases and subscriptions

When you view, purchase, restore or use a membership subscription, the App uses Apple’s StoreKit services and may handle information returned by Apple, including:

  1. Product name, price, currency, subscription period, availability and entitlement status;
  2. Purchase, restore-purchase or subscription-status-refresh requests;
  3. Apple-verified transactions, product identifiers, transaction states and current entitlement states, so that the App can provide applicable membership features;
  4. A local display of subscribed, not subscribed or another entitlement state based on Apple’s response.

Your payment account, Apple Account credentials, bank-card number, payment password, complete payment credentials and refund information are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy and terms. The App does not obtain your Apple Account password, payment password, full bank-card number or complete payment credentials.

2.3 Game state used during a session

During a game session, the App may temporarily process in device memory:

  1. Current score, combo count, current fruit and next fruit;
  2. Fruit position, movement, collision, merging and game-over state;
  3. Session start time, session duration, largest merged fruit and settlement information.

This information is used to run the game, display the interface and produce the settlement. The current version does not upload or synchronize it to the cloud. Temporary state may be cleared after game over, restart, termination of the App or system reclamation.

2.4 Audio playback and haptic feedback

The App may play background music and use iOS audio playback and haptic feedback:

  1. The App does not record, identify or collect your voice through the microphone;
  2. The App does not upload audio playback content to us;
  3. iOS may handle audio-session, device-audio or system-diagnostic information under Apple’s own rules.

2.5 Feedback and requests you voluntarily submit

If you contact us by email or through the App Store “App Support” channel, we may handle information you choose to provide, including:

  1. Your email address or other contact details;
  2. Your description of a feature, game-rule, score or privacy-rights issue;
  3. The App version, device model, operating-system version, reproduction steps, screenshots or screen recordings you choose to provide;
  4. Necessary information you voluntarily provide to verify a request or troubleshoot a problem.

We use this information only to respond, troubleshoot, correct game content, handle intellectual-property notices, respond to privacy-rights requests or comply with legal obligations. We do not ask for sensitive information unrelated to the issue.

2.6 Technical information handled by system and Apple services

The App itself does not actively read your advertising identifier, precise location, contacts, photos, camera content, microphone recordings, health data or motion data, and does not actively create an analytics service.

When you use the App Store, “App Support” or email services to contact the developer, Apple, email providers or network-service providers may handle device, account, network or diagnostic technical information required to provide those services, such as device and system information, network-connection information, request time or error information. That processing is performed by the relevant provider under its own rules and is not the same as active collection by the App.

3. Permissions, Sensors and Device Content

The current version does not need camera, photos, microphone, contacts, precise location, Bluetooth, health or motion data to provide core gameplay and does not request permissions to read that content.

If you voluntarily choose a photo, screen recording or other file as an attachment in an email or App Store support process, that attachment is submitted by you and is handled under the rules of your email service, Apple service and the relevant provider. Remove unrelated sensitive content before submitting it.

4. Purposes and Principles of Processing

We handle information only as necessary for the following purposes:

  1. Saving and displaying the local best score;
  2. Calculating and displaying the local daily free-play allowance;
  3. Running fruit generation, movement, collision, merging, scoring and settlement during a session;
  4. Playing background music, providing haptic feedback and maintaining the game experience;
  5. Responding to inquiries, bug reports, intellectual-property notices and privacy-rights requests you submit;
  6. Maintaining App security, troubleshooting, handling complaints and complying with legal obligations.

We do not handle information for purposes unrelated to the above. We do not collect information through misleading, fraudulent, coercive or default-consent methods, and we will not refuse core gameplay merely because you do not agree to information processing unrelated to the App.

5. What We Do Not Currently Do

The current version:

  1. Does not create or require an App account;
  2. Does not upload or synchronize best scores, session scores, combos, session duration, game records or device settings to the cloud;
  3. Does not provide online leaderboards, social publishing, online matches, friend systems or communication with strangers;
  4. Does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, user profiling, cross-app tracking or personalized advertising;
  5. Does not collect payment accounts, bank-card numbers, payment passwords or complete payment credentials through the App; Apple handles StoreKit information under Apple’s rules;
  6. Does not actively read contacts, photos, camera content, microphone recordings, precise location, facial information, health information or motion information;
  7. Does not sell personal information or provide it to third parties for advertising, marketing or purposes unrelated to the App.

6. Sharing, Entrusted Processing, Transfer and Disclosure

  1. We do not sell your personal information or share, transfer or publicly disclose it for purposes unrelated to the App.
  2. If you use email, App Store “App Support” or another third-party service to contact us, that service provider handles the information you provide under its own rules.
  3. Except with your consent or where required by laws, regulations, courts or administrative authorities, we do not provide feedback information you voluntarily submit to other parties.
  4. If a merger, division, dissolution, liquidation or another event requires a transfer of relevant information, we will take the measures required by law and notify you appropriately.
  5. The current version has no developer-controlled third-party advertising, analytics, cloud-storage or game-data processing service.

7. Retention, Deletion and Local Data

  1. The local best score, language choice, daily-play count/date and other local settings normally remain until you delete the App, clear its data or replace the relevant setting. The current version has no game-data copy controlled by us in the cloud.
  2. Game-session state is mainly held in memory and is normally cleared after game over, restart, termination of the App or system reclamation.
  3. Deleting the App or clearing its local data may remove the best score and other local records and they may not be recoverable.
  4. Feedback and privacy-rights request information you submit is normally kept until the relevant issue, request or dispute is resolved. Where the law requires continued retention, we keep it as required.
  5. When the retention period ends, we delete, anonymize or otherwise handle the information as permitted by law.

8. Information Security

  1. Because the current version mainly runs locally and does not upload game records, we do not maintain a developer server for storing game data.
  2. We use reasonable access controls, least-access practices, device protections and internal-management measures appropriate to the nature and context of the information.
  3. You should protect your device and email account and should not send passwords, verification codes or other sensitive information to anyone.
  4. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-information security incident occurs or may occur, we will take remedial measures and notify or report to relevant parties to the extent required by law.
  5. If you discover an App or contact-channel security issue, report it privately through “Contact Us”. Do not publish details that could expose personal information or system vulnerabilities.

Where provided by applicable law, you may contact us to:

  1. Learn whether we process your personal information and how our rules work;
  2. Access, copy, correct or supplement information you voluntarily submitted to us;
  3. Request deletion of information you voluntarily submitted to us;
  4. Withdraw consent for consent-based processing;
  5. Ask questions, make a complaint or report a concern about personal-information processing;
  6. Submit a request regarding children’s personal information or other specially protected information.

For the best score, language choice and game settings stored only on your device, delete the App or clear its data. For information held by an email provider or Apple, contact the relevant email provider, Apple Account or Apple Support.

  4. If a request cannot be confirmed, is outside our control, is repetitive, clearly unreasonable or may harm another person’s lawful rights, we will explain the reason. Applicable law controls where it provides otherwise.
  5. We will handle a complete request as soon as reasonably practicable and respond within the period required by law. For information directly controlled by Apple, an email provider or another service provider, we will explain the appropriate channel.
