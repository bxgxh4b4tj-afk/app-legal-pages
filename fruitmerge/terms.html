1. Definitions and Service Scope

  1. “App” means the DRAYAYA: Raise a Dragon Fruit iOS application and its software, interface, game assets, rules, audio, copy and related services.
  2. The App is a casual fruit-merging game. Its main features include:
  • moving and dropping the current fruit inside the container;
  • merging fruits after matching fruits collide under the game rules;
  • earning a score through space management, consecutive merges and combos;
  • displaying the current score, best score, next fruit, largest merged fruit and session duration;
  • viewing a settlement after game over and starting another session;
  • switching between Simplified Chinese and English where the App provides a language setting.
  1. The current version includes game elements such as cherry, strawberry, orange, lemon, apple, kiwi, pear, grape, peach, melon, watermelon and rainbow fruit. Actual assets, names and merge rules are those displayed in the App.
