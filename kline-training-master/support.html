Contact Us

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Thank you for using chompleek. This page is for product support, simulated-training feedback, feature issues, subscription questions, intellectual-property notices and privacy-rights requests.

1. Contact Channels

Email: sunny8060@foxmail.com

You may also use the “App Support” channel shown on the chompleek App Store product page. The developer name, developer entity and current contact details are those displayed by the App Store.

This in-App page and the document pages only display contact information. They do not read your email, contacts or other device content. If your device has no configured mail app, please use the App Store “App Support” channel.

2. Information That Helps Us Assist You

Depending on the issue, please provide only what is necessary:

  1. Device model and iOS version;
  2. App version;
  3. Current App language (Simplified Chinese or English);
  4. The page, level or feature where the issue occurred;
  5. Reproduction steps, expected result and actual result;
  6. Screenshots or a screen recording without sensitive information;
  7. For subscription questions, only the necessary App Store order information—never a password or complete payment credential.

Provide only what is needed to handle the issue. Before sending, remove unrelated personal information, payment details, identity-document numbers and other sensitive content.

3. Feature or Interface Issues

Please describe:

  1. The page or button where the issue occurred;
  2. The action after which the issue first appeared;
  3. Whether the issue can be reproduced consistently;
  4. Whether it remains after restarting the App, changing the network or reinstalling;
  5. Your device model, iOS version and App version.

For crashes, freezes, layout problems or display issues, attach a screenshot or screen recording without sensitive content where possible.

4. Simulated Training, Candlestick or Indicator Issues

Please include, where possible:

  1. The level number or training stage;
  2. The relevant candlestick position, actions taken or settlement result;
  3. The indicator name, value or calculation step you believe is incorrect;
  4. The content currently displayed and your suggested correction;
  5. A public, reliable reference if one is available.

Candlesticks, indicators, returns, scores and training results in the App are simulated practice content and do not represent live market data, real trading results or investment advice.

5. Subscriptions, Billing, Restore and Refunds

  1. View or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  2. Restore an existing membership entitlement: open the App and use “Restore Subscription” on the subscription page.
  3. Request a refund or report an unrecognized charge: use Apple’s “Report a Problem” page or contact Apple Support.
  4. Deleting the App, clearing local data or changing devices does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription.
  5. If an entitlement does not appear after a restore, provide the device model, App version, steps taken and the message returned by Apple. Do not send your Apple Account password, payment password or full bank-card number.

Payment, refunds, auto-renewal, cancellation and transaction records are handled by Apple under Apple’s rules. We can help locate an App-side issue, but Apple decides whether a refund is approved.

6. Privacy and Personal-Information Requests

Through the email above, you may request:

  1. Information about whether we process your personal information;
  2. Access to, a copy of, correction of or supplementation to information you submitted;
  3. Deletion of information you submitted;
  4. Withdrawal of consent for consent-based processing;
  5. Action regarding children’s personal information;
  6. Review of an information-security issue, complaint or report.

We suggest the subject “Privacy Rights Request — chompleek”. Describe the request, information involved and a usable contact method. To prevent impersonation, we may verify information reasonably related to the request. We will handle a complete request as soon as reasonably practicable and respond within the period required by law.

For training progress and settings stored only on your device, delete the App or clear its data. For purchase, payment and subscription information held by Apple, use your Apple Account, Apple Support or Apple’s privacy tools.

7. Intellectual-Property or Content Notice

If you believe the App’s software, interface, copy, charts, training content or other materials infringe your lawful rights, include:

  1. Your name or entity name and valid contact details;
  2. The rights you claim and proof of ownership;
  3. The specific content, page or location at issue;
  4. The facts and reasons supporting your notice;
  5. The action you ask us to take.

Please ensure the information is true, accurate and complete. We will review and handle the notice reasonably.

8. Security Issues

If you find an issue that may affect user information, subscription information or App security, report it privately by email and provide, where possible:

  1. A description of the issue and its impact;
  2. Reproduction steps or necessary verification information;
  3. The discovery time and a contact method;
  4. Any suggested fix.

Do not publicly disclose vulnerability details, user information, Apple Account information or payment credentials. Do not use a security issue to access, modify, delete or distribute another person’s information.

9. Please Do Not Send

Do not send:

  1. Apple Account passwords, email passwords, verification codes or payment passwords;
  2. Full bank-card numbers, complete payment credentials or security codes;
  3. Original identity documents, facial photos, precise location or personal information unrelated to the issue;
  4. Another person’s personal information, account information or unauthorized screenshots;
  5. Large amounts of unrelated files, advertising, marketing or unlawful content.

We will never ask for your Apple Account password, payment password or full bank-card number through the App.

10. Processing Notes

We will process complete requests as soon as reasonably practicable. Timing may depend on reproduction difficulty, Apple transaction status, completeness of the materials and legal requirements. For privacy requests that must be handled under law, we will respond within the legally required period. If an issue is controlled by Apple or another provider and cannot be handled by us directly, we will explain the appropriate channel.
