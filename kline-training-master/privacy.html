chompleek Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026 · Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how chompleek (the "App") handles information about you. The developer information is shown on the App Store product page (the "Developer", "we", "us" or "our"). This Policy applies when you use the App or voluntarily contact us through the available support channels.

We value personal-information and privacy protection. Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. If you do not agree with it, stop using the App.

1. Current Data-Handling Overview

  1. The current version does not require an account and does not provide a developer-controlled account system, social relationships, shared leaderboard data or cloud synchronization for training data.
  2. The current version uses built-in simulated training scenarios and candlestick data. The App does not connect to securities, futures, funds, digital-asset or other trading accounts, place real orders or read real investment-account information.
  3. The current version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, user profiling, cross-app tracking or personalized advertising services.
  4. Except for Apple system services required for StoreKit purchases, restore purchases and entitlement verification, the App does not actively upload your training data, settings or device content to a server controlled by us.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Local training progress and App settings

The App may store the following locally on your device through iOS local storage:

  1. Your current level, level progress, training results, rank, current and total experience points;
  2. Your total training count, daily training count and relevant date, to apply in-App training and experience-point rules;
  3. App language, candlestick color mode, chart display preferences, interface settings and other local settings;
  4. The local display state of purchased benefits and local state used after a restore-purchase result;
  5. If a particular development or test build exposes debug parameters, those may also be stored only on the device.

The current version does not actively upload this information to us, use it for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking, or make it public to other users.

2.2 Apple StoreKit purchase information

When you view, purchase, restore or use the one-time purchase product, the App uses Apple's StoreKit services and may handle:

  1. Product name, price, currency, availability and entitlement status returned by Apple;
  2. Purchase or restore-purchase requests;
  3. Apple-verified transactions, product identifiers, transaction states and current entitlement states;
  4. A local display of "Purchased", "Not Purchased" or another entitlement state based on Apple's response.

Your payment account, Apple Account credentials, bank-card number, payment password, complete payment credentials and refund information handled by Apple are processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and terms.

2.3 Feedback and requests you voluntarily submit

If you contact us by email or through the App Store "App Support" channel, we may handle information you choose to provide, including your email address, description of a feature or training issue, purchase question, privacy-rights request, App version, device model, screenshots or recordings you choose to provide.

We use this information only to respond, troubleshoot, correct training content, handle purchase issues, process intellectual-property notices, respond to privacy-rights requests or comply with legal obligations.

2.4 Technical information handled by system and Apple services

The App itself does not create a developer-controlled account system or analytics service, and does not actively read your advertising identifier, precise location, contacts, photos, camera content, microphone recordings or health data.

When you use the App Store, StoreKit or App Store "App Support", Apple and network-service providers may handle device, account, network, transaction or diagnostic technical information required to provide those services.

3. Permissions, Sensors and Device Content

The current version does not need camera, photos, microphone, contacts, precise location, Bluetooth, health or motion data to provide its core features, and does not request permissions to read that content.

4. Purposes and Principles of Processing

We handle information only as necessary to preserve local training progress, provide simulated training and restore purchases, respond to your inquiries, maintain App security, and comply with applicable law.

5. What We Do Not Currently Do

The current version does not create an App account, cloud-sync training data, connect to real trading accounts, include advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal information, or read contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, facial, health or motion data.

6. Sharing, Entrusted Processing, Transfer and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Purchases and restore purchases require interaction with Apple StoreKit, handled under Apple's own Privacy Policy and terms. If you use email or App Store support to contact us, that provider handles your information under its own rules.

7. Retention, Deletion and Local Data

Local training progress and settings remain on your device until you delete the App. Deleting the App does not delete purchase or transaction records held by Apple.

8. Information Security

We use reasonable access controls and device protections. Protect your device, Apple Account and email account; do not send passwords, verification codes or payment credentials to anyone.

9. Your Personal-Information Rights

You may contact us to access, copy, correct or delete information you voluntarily submitted, or to make other rights requests. For information held by Apple, use your Apple Account or Apple Support.

10. Contact Us

To exercise privacy rights or ask questions about this Policy, email sunny8060@foxmail.com or use the "App Support" channel on the App Store product page.
