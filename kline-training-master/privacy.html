chompleek Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026 Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how chompleek (the “App”) handles information about you. The developer information is shown on the App Store product page (the “Developer”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This Policy applies when you use the App or voluntarily contact us through the available support channels.

We value personal-information and privacy protection. We handle information according to the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, necessity, good faith, transparency, specified purposes and data minimization. Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. If you do not agree with it, stop using the App.

1. Current Data-Handling Overview

  1. The current version does not require an account and does not provide a developer-controlled account system, social relationships, shared leaderboard data or cloud synchronization for training data.
  2. The current version uses built-in simulated training scenarios and candlestick data. The App does not connect to securities, futures, funds, digital-asset or other trading accounts, place real orders or read real investment-account information.
  3. The current version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, user profiling, cross-app tracking or personalized advertising services.
  4. Except for Apple system services required for StoreKit purchases, restore purchases and membership-entitlement verification, the App does not actively upload your training data, settings or device content to a server controlled by us.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Local training progress and App settings

To preserve your state, the App may store the following locally on your device through iOS local storage:

  1. Your current level, level progress, training results, rank, current experience points and total experience points;
  2. Your total training count, daily training count and relevant date, to apply in-App training and experience-point rules;
  3. App language, candlestick color mode, chart display preferences, interface settings and other local settings;
  4. The local display state of membership benefits and local state used after a restore-purchase result;
  5. If a particular development or test build exposes interface-debug parameters, those parameters may also be stored only on the device.

This information is used to preserve progress, provide training, display feedback, apply daily rules and remember settings. The current version does not actively upload it to us, use it for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking, or make it public to other users.

2.2 Apple StoreKit purchases and subscription information

When you view, purchase, restore or use a membership subscription, the App uses Apple’s StoreKit services and may handle the following information returned by Apple:

  1. Product name, price, currency, subscription period, availability and entitlement status;
  2. Purchase, restore-purchase or subscription-status-refresh requests;
  3. Apple-verified transactions, product identifiers, transaction states, revocation states and current entitlement states, so that the App can provide applicable membership features;
  4. A local display of “Subscribed”, “Not Subscribed” or another entitlement state based on Apple’s response.

Your payment account, Apple Account credentials, bank-card number, payment password, complete payment credentials and refund information handled by Apple are processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy and terms. The App does not obtain your Apple Account password, payment password, full bank-card number or complete payment credentials. Apple’s handling is not controlled solely by this Policy.

2.3 Feedback and requests you voluntarily submit

If you contact us by email or through the App Store “App Support” channel, we may handle information you choose to provide, including:

  1. Your email address or other contact details;
  2. Your description of a feature issue, simulated-training issue, subscription issue or privacy-rights request;
  3. The App version, device model, operating-system version, reproduction steps, screenshots or screen recordings you choose to provide;
  4. Necessary information you voluntarily provide to verify a request, troubleshoot a problem or locate a subscription issue.

We use this information only to respond, troubleshoot, correct training content, handle subscription issues, process intellectual-property notices, respond to privacy-rights requests or comply with legal obligations. We do not ask you to provide sensitive information unrelated to the issue.

2.4 Technical information handled by system and Apple services

The App itself does not create a developer-controlled account system or analytics service, and it does not actively read your advertising identifier, precise location, contacts, photos, camera content, microphone recordings or health data.

When you use the App Store, StoreKit, Apple subscription management or App Store “App Support”, Apple and network-service providers may handle device, account, network, transaction or diagnostic technical information required to provide those services, such as device and system information, network-connection information, request time, transaction identifiers or error information. That processing is performed by the relevant service provider under its own rules and is not the same as active collection by the App.

3. Permissions, Sensors and Device Content

The current version does not need camera, photos, microphone, contacts, precise location, Bluetooth, health or motion data to provide its core features, and does not request permissions to read that content.

If you voluntarily choose a photo, screen recording or other file as an attachment in an email or App Store support process, that attachment is submitted by you and is handled under the rules of your email service, Apple service and the relevant provider. Remove unrelated sensitive content before submitting it.

4. Purposes and Principles of Processing

We handle information only as necessary for the following purposes:

  1. Preserving local training progress, ranks, experience points, daily training state and App settings;
  2. Providing simulated candlestick training, indicator displays, settlement, membership features and restore purchases;
  3. Responding to inquiries, issue reports, subscription questions and privacy-rights requests that you submit;
  4. Maintaining App security, troubleshooting, handling complaints and processing intellectual-property notices;
  5. Complying with applicable laws, regulations, regulatory requirements or lawful requests from courts or administrative authorities.

We do not handle information for purposes unrelated to the above. We do not collect information through misleading, fraudulent, coercive or default-consent methods, and we will not refuse core training features merely because you do not agree to information processing unrelated to the App.

5. What We Do Not Currently Do

The current version:

  1. Does not create or require an App account;
  2. Does not upload or synchronize your training progress, ranks, experience points, daily training records or settings to the cloud;
  3. Does not connect to real trading accounts, place orders, transfer funds or collect real investment-account information;
  4. Does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, user profiling or cross-app tracking;
  5. Does not sell personal information or provide it to third parties for their advertising, marketing or unrelated purposes;
  6. Does not actively read contacts, photos, camera content, microphone recordings, precise location, facial information, health information or motion information.

6. Sharing, Entrusted Processing, Transfer and Disclosure

  1. We do not sell your personal information or share, transfer or publicly disclose it for purposes unrelated to the App.
  2. Purchases, restore purchases, subscription management and entitlement verification require interaction with Apple StoreKit or related Apple services. This is necessary to complete your request, and Apple handles the information under Apple’s own Privacy Policy and terms.
  3. If you use email, App Store “App Support” or another third-party service to contact us, that service provider handles the information you provide under its own rules.
  4. Except with your consent or where required by laws, regulations, courts or administrative authorities, we do not provide feedback information you voluntarily submit to other parties.
  5. If a merger, division, dissolution, liquidation or another event requires a transfer of relevant information, we will take the measures required by law and notify you appropriately.

7. Retention, Deletion and Local Data

  1. Local training progress and App settings normally remain until you delete the App, clear its data or replace the relevant settings. The current version has no cloud copy of training data controlled by us.
  2. Deleting the App or clearing its local data may remove training progress, ranks, experience points and settings from the device. Purchase, subscription, payment and transaction records held by Apple are not automatically deleted when the App is deleted.
  3. Feedback and privacy-rights request information you submit is normally kept until the relevant issue, request or dispute is resolved. Where the law requires continued retention, we keep it as required.
  4. When the retention period ends, we delete, anonymize or otherwise handle the information as permitted by law, except where law requires otherwise or Apple retains information under Apple’s rules.

8. Information Security

  1. We use reasonable access controls, least-access practices, device protections, transmission security and internal-management measures appropriate to the nature and context of the information.
  2. You should protect your device, Apple Account and email account and should not send passwords, verification codes, payment credentials or other sensitive information to anyone.
  3. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-information security incident occurs or may occur, we will take remedial measures and notify or report to relevant parties to the extent required by law.
  4. If you discover an App or contact-channel security issue, report it privately through “Contact Us”. Do not publish details that could expose user information, transaction information or system vulnerabilities.

9. Your Personal-Information Rights

Where provided by applicable law, you may contact us to:

  1. Learn whether we process your personal information and how our rules work;
  2. Access, copy, correct or supplement information you voluntarily submitted to us;
  3. Request deletion of information you voluntarily submitted or, where legally available, deletion of related processing records;
  4. Withdraw consent for consent-based processing;
  5. Ask questions, make a complaint or report a concern about personal-information processing;
  6. Submit a request regarding children’s personal information or other specially protected information.

For training progress and settings stored only on your device, delete the App or clear its data. For purchase, payment and subscription information held by Apple, use your Apple Account, Apple Support or Apple’s privacy tools.

10. How We Handle Rights Requests

  1. Send a request to sunny8060@foxmail.com; we suggest the subject “Privacy Rights Request — chompleek”.
  2. To help us verify and process it, describe the request, information involved, contact details and necessary facts. Do not send your Apple Account password, payment password, full bank-card number, an original identity document or unrelated sensitive material.
  3. To prevent impersonation, fraud or harm to another person’s rights, we may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity. We will not ask for sensitive information unrelated to verification.
  4. If a request cannot be confirmed, is outside our control, is repetitive, clearly unreasonable or may harm another person’s lawful rights, we will explain the reason. Applicable law controls where it provides otherwise.
  5. We will handle a complete request as soon as reasonably practicable and respond within the period required by law. For information directly controlled by Apple, we will explain how to submit the request to Apple.

11. Children and Children’s Information

  1. The App does not require an account and does not actively collect a child’s name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, recordings, facial information or real investment-account information.
  2. Children under 14 should use the App under a parent or legal guardian’s guidance. A parent or legal guardian should decide or guide any membership purchase, payment, email or feedback submission involving screenshots or recordings.
  3. If a parent or legal guardian believes a child submitted unnecessary personal information, they may contact us to request access, correction, deletion or another lawful action.
  4. If we discover that we processed a child’s personal information without the legally required guardian consent, we will take deletion, processing restrictions or other remedial measures to the extent required by law.

12. Third-Party Services and External Rules

  1. The App Store, Apple StoreKit, Apple subscription management, Apple Support and Apple refund services are provided by Apple. Your use of those services is also subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy, Media Services Terms and other applicable rules.
  2. When you contact us by email, your email provider may handle the address, message, attachments, sending time and network technical information. Review the content before sending it.
  3. The current version has no developer-controlled third-party advertising, analytics, social login, cloud storage or user-profiling service. If we add one, we will update this Policy before launch to describe the service, information categories and purposes.

13. Cross-Border Processing

  1. The current version does not upload training progress, settings or device content to a server outside our control.
  2. Apple, email providers and App Store-related services may process information generated through their services using global infrastructure and their own privacy rules. Their storage locations, cross-border arrangements and retention periods are governed by the relevant provider’s published rules.
  3. If we later add a feature that requires us to provide personal information outside the jurisdiction, we will separately inform you of the purpose, information scope, recipient, retention, rights and security measures and complete legally required procedures.

14. Updates to This Policy

  1. If the purposes, methods, scope, third-party services, retention periods or your rights materially change, we will update this Policy and notify you through an in-App page, release notes, the update date on the document page or another method required by law.
  2. Where a material change legally requires renewed consent, we will obtain it as required and will not replace legally required consent with default continued use.
  3. You can view the current version of this Privacy Policy and its update date in the App’s Settings page at any time.

To exercise privacy rights or ask questions about this Policy, information security or the App’s data handling, email sunny8060@foxmail.com through the in-App “Contact Us” page, or use the “App Support” channel shown on the App Store product page.
